Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about finding work in Essex

How do I find jobs in Essex without going to London?

Essex Job Board focuses exclusively on roles based within Essex's 20 largest towns and cities, filtering out London-only positions automatically. You can also use the search and location filters to narrow results to your specific area, and apply the salary filter to compare local pay against your current commute costs.

What is the average salary in Essex?

This varies significantly by role and location. Entry-level retail and hospitality roles typically pay between £11 and £14 per hour. Office and administrative roles generally start from £22,000 to £26,000. Skilled trades, healthcare, and professional roles often range from £30,000 to £50,000 depending on experience and location, with Chelmsford and Brentwood typically offering the highest salaries due to proximity to London.

Which Essex towns have the most jobs?

Basildon, Chelmsford, Colchester, and Southend-on-Sea consistently have the highest number of vacancies due to their size and industrial bases. Basildon is particularly strong for logistics and warehousing, Chelmsford for office and professional roles, Colchester for healthcare and education, and Southend for retail and hospitality.

Do I need to register to apply for jobs?

No. Essex Job Board does not require any registration. Every listing links directly to the employer's application page or the original job posting, so you can apply immediately.

How often are new jobs added?

Listings are pulled and updated daily from live job board data, so new vacancies typically appear within 24 hours of being posted by employers.

Can I post a job on Essex Job Board?

Yes. Visit the Post a Job page to see our current listing options for employers and recruiters.

Are remote jobs included?

Yes, where available. You can use the Remote filter on any location page to see remote-eligible roles alongside local listings.

Jobs in Essex 2026: What's Hiring Right Now

Published June 2026 · Essex Job Board

Essex is one of the fastest-growing employment markets outside London, with over 20,000 live vacancies across the county at any given time. From logistics hubs in Basildon to tech firms in Chelmsford, the variety of roles available in 2026 is broader than ever.

Logistics & Warehousing

The expansion of distribution centres along the A12 and M25 corridors has driven consistent demand for warehouse operatives, HGV drivers, and logistics coordinators. Major employers include Amazon, Ocado, and DHL, all of whom maintain large operations across Grays, Basildon, and Harlow.

Healthcare & NHS

Mid and South Essex NHS Foundation Trust is one of the county's largest employers, with ongoing recruitment across Chelmsford, Southend, and Basildon. Nursing, allied health, and administrative roles are consistently available.

IT & Tech

Chelmsford has emerged as a regional tech hub, with a growing cluster of software, cybersecurity, and digital marketing firms. Colchester's proximity to the University of Essex also supports a vibrant graduate employment market.

Best Places to Find Work in Essex

Published June 2026 · Essex Job Board

Not all parts of Essex offer the same job opportunities. Here's a breakdown of the best locations for job seekers in 2026.

Chelmsford

As the county town and a city since 2012, Chelmsford offers the widest range of professional, office, and public sector roles. It's well-connected to London Liverpool Street (35 minutes) making it attractive to employers who want talent without London costs.

Colchester

Home to one of the UK's largest garrisons and a major university, Colchester has strong demand in healthcare, education, retail, and defence-adjacent industries. The town centre has seen significant regeneration in recent years.

Basildon & Southend

These two towns dominate South Essex employment. Basildon's industrial estates host major logistics and manufacturing operations, while Southend's seafront economy drives hospitality, retail, and tourism jobs year-round.

Harlow

A new town with deep roots in science and manufacturing, Harlow is home to several major pharmaceutical and medical device companies including GSK and Abbott. Science, engineering, and production roles are consistently strong here.

How to Find a Job in Essex Without Going to London

Published June 2026 · Essex Job Board

For years, Essex workers have accepted long commutes into London as the price of a decent salary. But the post-pandemic shift toward hybrid and local working has changed that calculus significantly.

Use local job boards

National job boards like Indeed and Reed are dominated by London roles. Using a local Essex-specific board means every listing is genuinely reachable. Essex Job Board focuses exclusively on the 20 largest locations in the county, filtering out irrelevant results automatically.

Target Essex-based employers directly

Many of Essex's largest employers — NHS trusts, councils, logistics firms, manufacturers — recruit primarily through their own websites and local channels rather than national boards. Bookmark their careers pages directly.

Consider the new towns

Harlow and Basildon were purpose-built to provide employment outside London. Both have strong industrial and commercial bases that are often overlooked by job seekers who focus on Chelmsford or Southend.

Use salary filters wisely

Essex salaries are typically 15-25% lower than equivalent London roles, but so is the cost of living. A £35k role in Chelmsford often represents better quality of life than a £45k role in the City once commuting costs are factored in.

Jobs in Chelmsford 2026: A Complete Guide

Published June 2026 · Essex Job Board

As the county town of Essex and officially a city since 2012, Chelmsford offers one of the widest ranges of employment opportunities in the region. With excellent rail links to London Liverpool Street in around 35 minutes, it attracts both local employers and businesses looking for talent without London overheads.

What's hiring in Chelmsford

Office and administrative roles dominate the city centre, supported by a growing legal and financial services sector. Anglia Ruskin University's Chelmsford campus also drives demand for education and graduate-level roles. Retail and hospitality remain strong around the High Chelmer and Bond Street shopping areas.

Salary expectations

Office-based roles in Chelmsford typically range from £24,000 for entry-level admin positions to £45,000 plus for experienced professionals in finance, legal, and management. Hourly retail and hospitality roles generally sit between £11 and £14 per hour.

Getting around

Chelmsford's compact city centre means most jobs are within easy walking distance of the train station and bus interchange. For those without a car, this makes it one of the most accessible employment hubs in Essex.

Jobs in Basildon 2026: Logistics, Industry and Beyond

Published June 2026 · Essex Job Board

Basildon is one of the largest employment centres in South Essex, built around an extensive network of industrial estates and business parks along the A127 and A13 corridors. As one of the original post-war new towns, it was designed from the ground up with employment in mind.

What's hiring in Basildon

Logistics and warehousing dominate, with major distribution centres for national retailers and courier firms based across Basildon's industrial estates. Manufacturing also remains a significant employer, alongside a growing customer service and contact centre sector. Retail roles are plentiful around the Eastgate Shopping Centre and Festival Leisure Park.

Salary expectations

Warehouse and logistics roles typically start from £11.50 to £13 per hour, rising significantly for supervisory and management positions. Skilled trades and manufacturing roles often pay £28,000 to £38,000 depending on experience.

Why Basildon works for job seekers

With direct rail access to London Fenchurch Street and strong road links via the A127 and A13, Basildon offers genuine flexibility — whether you're looking for local work or an easier commute into the capital.

Jobs in Colchester 2026: Britain's Oldest Recorded Town

Published June 2026 · Essex Job Board

Colchester combines a historic town centre with a modern, diverse economy. Home to one of the UK's largest army garrisons and the University of Essex, the town benefits from a young population and a steady flow of graduate talent entering the local job market each year.

What's hiring in Colchester

Healthcare and education are major employers, supported by Colchester General Hospital and the university itself. Defence-adjacent roles are common given the garrison presence, while the town centre regeneration has brought new retail, hospitality, and leisure opportunities. Construction and trades remain in steady demand as development continues.

Salary expectations

Healthcare assistant and support roles typically start around £22,000 to £25,000, while skilled trades such as electricians and site supervisors can earn £35,000 to £45,000 or more. Graduate roles linked to the university sector vary widely depending on the field.

Student and graduate opportunities

With a large student population, Colchester also has strong demand for part-time and flexible roles in retail, hospitality, and tutoring — ideal for those balancing study with work.

Jobs in Southend-on-Sea 2026: Seafront, Retail and Beyond

Published June 2026 · Essex Job Board

Southend-on-Sea is Essex's largest seaside town and one of its biggest employment centres, with a city status granted in 2022. Its economy spans a traditional seafront tourism industry alongside a growing professional services sector in the town centre.

What's hiring in Southend

Hospitality, retail, and tourism roles are abundant year-round, with seasonal peaks during summer months along the seafront and pier. The town centre also supports a healthy mix of office-based roles in finance, insurance, and local government. Southend Hospital is one of the area's largest employers for healthcare roles.

Salary expectations

Hospitality and retail roles typically range from £11 to £13 per hour, with supervisory positions reaching £24,000 to £28,000. Office and professional roles in the town centre generally start from £24,000, rising to £40,000 plus for experienced staff.

Transport links

Southend benefits from two railway stations with direct services to London Liverpool Street and Fenchurch Street, both under an hour, making it a popular choice for those wanting coastal living with London accessibility.
